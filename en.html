HogaClic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

HogaClic is an app for organising household chores, developed by Ana Peral García (Anusca Digital). This policy explains what data the app uses and the choices you have.

1. Data you keep in the app

Chores, profiles (name and, optionally, a photo or emoji), history, proof notes and photos, rewards and settings are stored only on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also stored in your private iCloud account, managed by Apple. We have no access to this data: there are no user accounts and no servers of our own.

2. Notifications and widget

Reminders are scheduled on the device itself. The widget reads a summary of your chores that is also stored on the device.

3. Photos

If you choose a photo for a profile or as proof of a chore, the app only accesses the photo you select and stores it on the device.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

5. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To do so, Google may collect the device’s advertising identifier, approximate location (derived from the IP address), the ads you see, your interactions with them, and performance and crash data. In the European Union we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads, and on iPhone the advertising identifier is only used if you allow it in Apple’s prompt. You can change your choice in the app’s Settings > Privacy options and in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Child profiles never show ads. More information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Children

Child profiles are created by an adult on their device. Their data does not leave the device (or iCloud, if enabled) and is not used for advertising.

7. Retention

Until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes the data from the device.

8. Your rights

You can view, change and delete your data in the app. For advertising data you can exercise your rights with Google. For any questions, write to anuscadigital@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will say so on this page with its new date.
